Hi, I'm Oleksandra Kaftanatii!

I’m studying for a Bachelor’s degree in Animation and Illustration at Anglia Ruskin University. Most of the time, I try to experiment with different things, mainly related to art, and step outside my comfort zone. I really love skeletons, I collect books and illustrations by other artists, and also love watching horror films and thrillers. I dream of bringing the stories I write in my spare time to life, and I plan to release a comic or my own indie animation in the future, once I’ve gained enough experience in the animation industry. That’s why I can’t wait for the chance to work with people in animation, to get to meet new people and gain experience.
